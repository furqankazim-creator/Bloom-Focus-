> Bloom Focus turns attention training into play. Children practise short, game-based sessions that adapt to them in real time. Parents start the day's training at home, watch their child improve, and stay in touch with the therapist through private messaging. Throughout, the child is cheered on by their favourite hero.

## 2. The problem we solve

*Use as a 3-column "Why Bloom Focus" section.*

1. **Practice stops at the clinic door.** Children see their therapist for a short time each week. What really builds skills is daily practice at home, and that is hard to keep going.
2. **One-size-fits-all games don't fit.** A task that is too hard frustrates a child, and one that is too easy bores them. Generic brain games don't know the child's age, needs or sensory profile.
3. **Parents and therapists lose touch between sessions.** Updates arrive by scattered texts and emails, and nobody sees the whole picture.

**Our answer:** "One app that connects the child, the parent and the therapist. It has games that adapt to every child, activities made by the therapist, and progress everyone can see."

## 3. Three apps in one

*A "Choose your side" section with 3 cards.*

### 🧒 For children: "Play, grow, shine"
- A warm, animated **start flow**. It first asks *"How are you feeling today?"* (6 feelings with 3D faces), then *"Have you done your training today?"*
- **Today's training**: 4 short focus games picked for them, played back to back with "Next game →".
- A **hero buddy** who plays alongside them, talks to them and cheers them on.
- A **Fun Zone** that unlocks after training as a reward: Bubble Pop, Hero Match and Connect the Dots.
- A **Calm Corner** that is always open: breathing with their hero when they feel tired, worried or grumpy.
- Stars, streaks and confetti that make progress feel exciting.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 For parents: "Know how they're doing"
- One big **"Let's go!"** button starts the day's training.
- **Progress** in plain language: skills improving, sessions played, streaks.
- **Activities from the therapist** appear automatically in the child's training.
- **Private messaging** with the therapist, with photos, screenshots, voice notes, documents and check-ins.
- A child profile they help keep up to date: favourite characters, sensory needs and more.

### 🩺 For therapists: "Your therapy plan, in their hands"
- A **Today** dashboard showing who to see, who practised and who needs attention.
- A **learning profile** for each child: age, IQ range, support needs, sensory needs and favourite characters.
- **Personalised suggestions** for the games and settings that suit each child.
- An **Activity Builder** for customising any training game: difficulty, length, hero and scene.
- **Game Studio** for making your own picture and word games in 4 simple steps.
- An **Activity Plan** for each child, with session explanations, dates and one-tap copy to another child.
- **Insights** showing skill trends over time.
- **Secure one-to-one messaging** with each family.

## 4. Feature highlights

*Alternate image-left and image-right rows with a phone mockup in each.*

### 4.1 Adaptive engine: "Always just right"
> Every game watches how the child is doing and adjusts as they play. When accuracy stays above 85%, it steps up. When it drops below 65%, it steps back. Children stay in their "just right" zone, challenged but never overwhelmed.

- Separate difficulty ladders for speed, distractions, memory span and more
- Session length adapts to the child's day
- Calm mode for sensitive days: gentler visuals and fewer distractions

## 5. How it works (3 steps)

*A horizontal 3-step strip with icons.*

1. **The therapist sets the plan.** They create the child's profile, choose or build activities, and pick their heroes.
2. **The child plays at home.** A parent taps "Let's go!" and the child checks in with how they feel, then plays today's 4 focus games with their hero.
3. **Everyone sees progress.** The games adapt automatically. Parents see progress, therapists see insights, and they stay in touch through messaging.

## 10. FAQ

**What ages is Bloom Focus for?**
Children aged about 4 to 12. Each game adapts to the child's level, not only their age.

**Do we need a therapist to use it?**
Bloom Focus works best when it is connected to a therapist, who personalises the plan and activities. Families can also follow the built-in daily training.

**How long is a session?**
About 4–10 minutes a day. Short, focused practice works best for children.

**What if my child has a hard day?**
Training starts with a feelings check-in. On tough days the Calm Corner is offered first, and calm mode makes games gentler.

**Can my child use their favourite character?**
Yes. They can choose up to 3 heroes from 40, or make their own hero from a photo.

**Can therapists make their own games?**
Yes. Game Studio lets therapists create picture puzzles, word searches, worksheets and more in 4 simple steps, using their own images.

**Is messaging private?**
Yes. Each conversation is one-to-one between a family and their own therapist, about one child.

**Which devices does it work on?**
iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, and web browsers.

**Is it a medical treatment?**
No. Bloom Focus supports therapy and home practice. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

## 12. Brand notes

- **Tone:** warm, encouraging and simple. Speak to parents as partners and to therapists as professionals.
- **Look:** light pastel backgrounds (sky blue, lavender, mint, peach), rounded cards, soft shadows and playful 3D characters.
- **Avoid:** clinical coldness, fear-based messaging ("Is your child falling behind?") and unproven medical claims.
- **Artwork:** the hero art uses Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D, which is MIT-licensed. Keep the licence notice in the website footer or credits page.
- **Accessibility:** good colour contrast, alt text on every image, and reduced-motion support for animations.
